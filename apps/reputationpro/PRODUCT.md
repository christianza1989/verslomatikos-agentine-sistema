# ReputationPro
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and persistent SQLite, specified in bridge/codex_task.md. Node.js 24 origin server. Owner subsequently authorized publication at https://reputacija.verslomatika.lt using Cloudflare Tunnel and Domenai.lt mail DNS verification on 2026-10-27. The publicly reachable edition remains an MVP demo, with unconfigured billing disabled.
## Users
Lithuanian clinic owners managing patient feedback; patients leaving a visit rating on a mobile phone.
## Product Purpose
Give one clinic a shareable review link and QR code, receive private feedback, and manage the feedback in a protected owner dashboard.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Landing offer EUR 49/month; 14-day Stripe trial. Rating 4–5 routes to configured Google Maps URL; 1–3 reveals private manager form. Google review link remains available at every rating. No claim that selective solicitation complies with Google policy. SQLite clients and internal_feedback tables. MVP has no SMS, AI replies, Gmail delivery or Google review ingestion.
## Evidence on Hand
bridge/codex_task.md and configs/reputation_saas.json are the business brief. Demo Vilniaus Denta is illustrative, not a verified customer. No testimonials or measured outcomes supplied. Stripe credentials and actual clinic review URL are not supplied.
## Brand Commitments
ReputationPro; Lithuanian copy. Owner delegated design, asset choices and implementation autonomously in chat.
## Product Principles
Keep patient forms short, make data private by default, distinguish local demo from real payments, never fabricate public review counts.
